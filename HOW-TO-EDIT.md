# How to update your website

Hi Milly! This is your website's home base. You don't need to know any code — you
just describe what you want in plain English and Claude makes it happen. Here's the
whole routine.

## The one important thing
**Always start Claude Code inside this folder** (`vital-kneads-site`). That's what
lets Claude automatically know everything about your site — your colors, fonts,
pages, and how to publish. If you start a blank chat somewhere else, it starts from
scratch and won't know any of this.

(The easiest way is the Claude Code desktop app, opened on this project folder.
Julian can set that up so it's one click.)

## How to make a change — every time
1. **Say what you want**, the way you'd tell a person. Examples:
   - "Shorten the intro paragraph on the home page and make it warmer."
   - "Swap the first photo on the About page for the new one in my Downloads."
   - "Add this testimonial: '…' — from Jane R., Oakland."
   - "Make the teal a little deeper, it feels too bright."
   - "Move the booking button above the photo on the Services page."
2. **Claude makes the change and shows you a preview** — a picture of how it looks —
   before anything goes public.
3. **You say yes or ask for tweaks.** Nothing is live until you approve.
4. **Claude publishes it**, and your real site at
   **https://vitalkneadsmt.com** updates within about a minute.

## Good to know
- **Nothing you do is permanent or risky.** Every change is saved as a version, so
  if you don't like something — even after it's live — Claude can undo it instantly.
  Feel free to experiment.
- **You can send Claude images** (new photos, a screenshot of something you like)
  and ask it to use or match them.
- **If something looks broken to *you* but Claude says it's fine**, it may just be
  your browser showing an old copy — try it on your phone, or ask Claude to
  double-check what's actually live.
- **Describe the feeling, not the code.** "Make it feel calmer / more elegant / less
  cramped" works great — Claude will handle the how.

## What Claude will always do for you
- Keep the site's look and brand consistent.
- Show you a preview before publishing.
- Only publish when you say so.
- Keep a version history so anything can be undone.

That's it. Open Claude Code here, describe what you'd like, look at the preview, and
say "publish it." 💆
